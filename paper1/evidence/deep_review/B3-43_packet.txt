                                                                                      these two are related since they both involve establishing a threshold

                                                                                      believed easier to be solved than probabilistic ﬁnancial risk as fewer
                                                                                      binary variables are included.
                                                                                          The inclusion of the most appropriate risk metric relies on the
                                                                                      willing of decision-maker. If the decision-maker is more concerned with
                                                                                      the variability of the results, the variability index should be used. This
                                                                                      risk measure only penalizes the solutions higher than the expectation.
                                                                                      While if he/she cannot tolerate the cost higher than a speciﬁc threshold,
                                                                                      probabilistic ﬁnancial risk or downside risk are suitable, because they
                                                                                      concentrate more on the threshold.

6.3. Comparisons of the risk metrics

    By analyzing the results obtained from the stochastic programming
model and the three risk management models, it can be found that risk
management always entails an increase of ETC in the CCS planning for
risk reduction. Nevertheless, inclusion of risk metrics to the optimal
deployment of CCS system under uncertainty can yield more robust
designs and each one has advantages and disadvantages.
    For the metric of variability index, it can be concluded that the
variability index is appropriate if the decision-maker prefers a solution
near the expectation. This risk metric is in linear representation, so the
programming problem can be solved more easily. Compared with the
formal variance, variability index seems more reasonable since the
