                                                                                           The results were validated by conducting the reservoir simulator-
                                                                                       based optimization using the DECE optimization algorithm. The
                                                                                       simulator-based optimization required 622 simulation runs to converge
                                                                                       to the optimum. On the other hand, ANN-based optimization achieved a
                                                                                       comparable solution with only 120 simulation runs, reducing the
                                                                                       number of simulation runs by 80.7%. The following are the conclusions
                                                                                       drawn from this study:

                                                                                       1) ANN-based proxy is well suited for the optimization problem because
                                                                                          of its accurate and fast predictions. Thousands of objective function
                                                                                          evaluations can be achieved in seconds. ANN offers an alternative for
                                                                                          tasks requiring many simulation runs.
                                                                                       2) Selection of the most informative input feature is paramount for
                                                                                          optimization study as irrelevant or non-informative features can
                                                                                          result in sub-optimal solutions.
                                                                                       3) Instead of using the permeability and porosity of well blocks indi­
                                                                                          vidually as input to the model to capture the effects of heterogeneity,
                                                                                          the inclusion of PP or average permeability and porosity along the
                                                                                          well as input increased the accuracy of the ANN model. PP and the
                                                                                          average permeability and porosity carry important information on
                                                                                          the flow and storage capacity while reducing the dimensionality of
