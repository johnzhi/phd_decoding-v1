That related DOI is not asserted as a verified identifier for every separately uploaded report or seismic file.

The complete supplied [IBDP terms](D6-07/IBDP%20-%20CO2%20DataShare%20Terms%20Final.pdf) are retained with the data. They permit use and sharing with attribution, prohibit sale of the licensed material, require retaining terms/identifier information, and prohibit misleading presentation. No endorsement by ISGS or US DOE is claimed. These terms are not replaced by a repository code licence.

Original files are unchanged. Searchable page text, extracted native cells and reference tables are derived representations for research, with locators and limitations recorded in the evidence register.
