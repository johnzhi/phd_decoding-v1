The equal-weight means and savings reproduce to the displayed precision. The cluster unit cost falls **37.37%** between scales, but its **relative advantage over standalone projects falls from 19.94% to 15.92%**. Statements about increasing cost-reduction potential must specify the metric. Multiplying a cost saving per tonne **avoided** by tonnes **captured** does not establish total financial savings.

Five of six plant-level savings agree with the printed costs after allowing display rounding. All three values remain distinguished.

## Physical routes, storage and utilities

The three direct plant-to-sink distances total **405.82 km**. Under an explicitly disclosed assumption of disjoint feeders plus one shared trunk, the listed feeder/trunk lengths total **233.39 km**, rather than the table's **228.23 km**. Omitting the Honghaiwan 5.05 km feeder gives **228.34 km**, still different. Routing, overlap and rounding cannot be reconstructed from the map.

The sum of three source-to-sink paths via the hub is **460.51 km** under the same assumption. A smaller unique physical network and larger per-source travel distance can coexist. These are accounting diagnostics, not optimized GIS routes, flow-weighted costs or proof of author-code errors. Ignored onshore cost and negligible hub capital are simplifying assumptions, not evidence of zero actual utilities, maintenance or permits. Figure 2's 100/200 m contours describe **water depth**, and its emissions legend literally includes **`>1000 Mt/a`**, retained unresolved.

Total-area aggregation, efficiency, density, pressure, well geometry and native annual injection limits are missing. One paragraph says **LF-21** instead of LF-2A.
