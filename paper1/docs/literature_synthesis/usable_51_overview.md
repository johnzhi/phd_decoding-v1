# What Paper 1 can use from the 51 detailed reviews

Prepared 22 November 2026 (Asia/Kuala_Lumpur). Evidence snapshot: `7e3a6f756d6fe2a73d71f39e41239d180d98c21f`, branch `research/paper-1-evidence-draft`, `johnzhi/phd_decoding-v1`.

**There is enough usable evidence to strengthen the argument, choose credible comparators and retain several bounded numerical benchmarks. There is not yet enough verified evidence for a complete regional lifecycle ranking.** The useful next step is to connect each claim or calculation to its actual role in Paper 1, rather than turn every reviewed paper into a model input.

The 51 detailed records cover 710 of the corpus's 1301 main-PDF pages, read as text and visually inspected. They include B1-01–B1-20, B2-01–B2-18, B3-01–B3-08, B3-11, B3-44, B4-05, B4-06 and B4-08. The other 41 publications have prior targeted reviews. None of the 92 is comprehensively complete under the expanded requirement to verify underlying cited sources, supplements and original models. The 3116 bibliography/dependency entries and 36 scoped original-passage checks are different quantities; neither means all those originals have been read or verified. One separately retained B4-06 supplement has three table and ten figure objects.

## 4. Recommended comparison set and contribution

The closest detailed-review comparisons are **B2-04** for reservoir/infrastructure coupling, **B4-06** for reservoir-constrained multiperiod matching, **B2-12** for multiperiod cost/GHG shipping and storage, **B2-08** for stochastic operating/buffer design, **B2-03/B2-09** for capacity/injectivity uncertainty, **B2-05/B2-06** for multimodal deployment, and **B2-13** for prospective transport and carbon payback. Foundational controls come from B3-11 and B3-44. These papers should be compared by boundary, decisions, time step, information, physical constraints, counterfactual and verification—not by an unqualified ranking of algorithms or quoted savings.

Four additional close comparisons are already identified among the targeted 41: **B3-14** for compensatory power and temporal limits, **B3-42** for yearly baseline emissions and domestic/foreign rollout, **B4-07** for resilience and expected carbon not stored, and **B3-23** for reevaluating proxy-selected decisions under a fuller simulator. **B3-29** already couples cargo dynamics and GHG, and **B3-37** provides the useful possibility that rate variation has little long-horizon effect in a tested reservoir. Their status remains targeted; they still matter for a cautious novelty statement.

An evidence-supported contribution statement is: *This study tests how resolving annual storage service and operational shipping, separately and jointly, changes the feasibility and conditional climate comparison of domestic, foreign and mixed pathways supplied by a common source. It reevaluates simplified-model decisions under a common detailed operating representation and makes parameter provenance, omitted inventory terms and geographic proxy limits explicit.*

This is a proposed positioning statement. It is not a claim of first-ever dynamic modelling, multimodal planning, reservoir coupling, compensatory generation, resilience assessment, proxy reevaluation or carbon-payback analysis. A [standalone proposed introduction](proposed_introduction.md) develops the argument in fresh wording with source locators. It is a revision candidate; the current manuscript is preserved.

## 5. Evidence still needed for the regional conclusion

No paper in the detailed 51 has yet yielded a verified, publicly complete pressure-constrained reservoir input/history dataset that closes Paper 1's annual maximum-service requirement. Pressure-aware methods and promising code/data leads are present; public availability, permission and reproduction differ. B2-15 explicitly says the underlying data cannot be shared. The advertised B2-18 assets and cited Fan dataset are unverified leads, not acquired datasets.

The detailed 51 likewise does not close one verified matched-case capture-to-ship inventory covering heat, electricity, compression, liquefaction, cooling, pretreatment and actual suppliers. B2-11 is a useful declared CHP process reference; B1-19/B1-20 supply partial output/accounting comparisons, not a complete facility balance.

Separate assets already retained outside this 92-paper corpus narrow those gaps: the Norwegian Bjarmeland deck has a 225 bar absolute BHP ceiling and 4951 paired simulated rate/pressure rows; CEMCAP provides three paired European MEA heat/work/compression cases; primary Tomakomai monthly reports and the EXCOOL trial supply selected observations. They are not additional rows in the 92-paper table. Bjarmeland still needs independent rerun/completion/warning/maximum-service checks. CEMCAP still needs compatibility with the selected ship boundary and actual supplier/pretreatment/asset treatment. Sparse monthly P/T, dated cumulative mass and a single closed-cargo voyage do not establish native injection telemetry, annual vessel availability or permanent geological retention. See [remaining work](../remaining_work.md), [primary follow-up](../primary_source_followup_review.md) and [public-source review](../public_reservoir_capture_review.md).
