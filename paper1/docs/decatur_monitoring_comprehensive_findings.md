The two depth windows1524–1981 m and1981–2195 m, B2 reference, fewer eastern shots and changing ground conditions all affect repeatability interpretation.

The paper reports15–20% NRMS in the assumed unaffected upper interval, with a growing lower-window anomaly west ofGM1. That supports a qualitative CO2-plume interpretation alongside other measurements. It is not15–20% saturation, a detection probability or retained mass. The paper does not print its exact NRMS equation, window or complete processing; its cited methodology remains unverified.

For an explicitly **analyst-defined conventional example**, let `NRMS = 200*RMS(b−a)/(RMS(a)+RMS(b))`. If `b=g*a` for positive gain g, this becomes `200*abs(g−1)/(1+g)`. Gains about1.16216 and1.22222 produce15% and20% respectively without assigning any reservoir mass. This demonstrates nonunique interpretation; it neither reproduces the actual survey metric nor attributes the real anomaly to amplitude gain. No author formula, saturation calibration or field signal is silently substituted.
