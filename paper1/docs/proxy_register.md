## Screened regional candidates

- Higashi-Niigata, Japan (B1-07): a named, history-matched simulation candidate. Relative volume improvement with pressure relief is available, but figures lack recoverable numerical vertical scales for the complete annual mass series. Do not create a 2030–2050 profile by assigning an arbitrary peak rate.
- Field X, Malaysia (B1-09 and B1-11): pressure constraints are available, but anonymised studies cannot establish that both refer to one site or that a commercial storage allocation exists.
- South-West Hub, Western Australia (B1-12): conceptual model provides relevant mechanisms, but its excluded thermal stress and model-specific assumptions prevent combining it with Malaysian pressure limits as one sink.
- Arun and regional aquifers (B4-08): named resource screening helps candidate selection. Resource magnitude is not yearly service, allocated capacity, readiness or access.
- B4-01 Malaysian power-sector scenarios identify potential local source context, but no matched Japanese plant inventory follows from national emissions estimates.

The numerical draft therefore uses an Indonesian retention benchmark and Norwegian process-burden scenarios openly. There is no calibrated Japan–Malaysia–Australia result. Future geographic labels should be attached only after compatible service profiles and inventories exist.
