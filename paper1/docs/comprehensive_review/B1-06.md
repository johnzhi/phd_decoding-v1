## Variables, units and assumptions

- SHmax/SHmin are horizontal-stress symbols in the sketch; Pw is wellbore pressure. P0 is present without a separate definition. Total/effective stresses, Biot convention, well orientation and rotations need clarification.

- Figure1 labels TOTSTRXX/pressure inpsi and TEMP indegF, with SSTVD depth. Negative-to-near-zero TEMP range suggests a change variable rather than absolute temperature; no temperature is recovered from it. TOTSTRXX-to-minimum-stress mapping and export alias need raw definitions.

- Stability plots separately label MD and TVDSS infeet. Measured depth is not the hydrostatic vertical depth from a known pressure reference. No multiplication of ppg by ftMD or unexplained TVDSS generates actual BHP.

- Pressure gradients are equivalents inppg. Analyst conversions explicitly assume pounds perUSgallon and standard gravity. They are not measured CO2 densities; surface pressure, fluid column, datum and temperature are unresolved.

- 2025 drilling11.3ppg, brine12.3ppg, injection13.8ppg and stopped pore-pressure gradient10.8ppg;2030 injection12.3/stopped11.75;2048 injection13.0/stopped12.45. Roles remain distinct.

- Page5 calls13.0ppg a pore-pressure gradient while Figure9 and surrounding method call it injection pressure gradient. Do not force wellbore and formation pressures equal or silently correct terminology.

- Cooling reductions78F in2030 and139F in2048 are temperature differences,43.3333/77.2222K. Converting an absolute Fahrenheit temperature would incorrectly subtract32.

- DoD is depth of damage, displayed with0/5/10%legend labels. Radius/diameter/other percentage denominator is unspecified. Colours indicate model damage modes/levels, not frequencies, outage duration or leakage probability.

- Depth intervals are approximate caption ftMD ranges. Continuous endpoint differences give spans; overlap is removed before a union. Point-only drilling breakouts remain points. Neither span nor union is rock volume, mass, fracture height or a rate fraction.

- Time labels2025/2030/2040/2048 are described as0/5/15/23years. Actual exported dates differ within those calendar years. They do not specify injection-start timestamps, ramping, rate history or stoppage duration/recurrence.

- I-3 is selected as marginally least stable during drilling among injectors. This deliberate selection is not a random sample or a statistical estimate for all wells.

- One-way THM coupling with advection/diffusion and thermoelastic stress effects is described. Grid, PVT, rock/thermal properties, stresses/orientations, strengths, stress path, boundaries, solver and validation are not supplied.

- The chosen perforated liner lacks detailed dimensions, metallurgy, cement/zonal isolation, perforation schedule, hydraulic losses, capacity/cost and endurance validation. Selection is not proof of permanent containment or universal superiority.
