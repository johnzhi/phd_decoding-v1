- L plant operational lifetime is not numerically specified in main PDF.25-year deployment horizon is distinct; full-life commitments can extend beyond2050.

- CI is initial investment NPV; CO lifetime operating NPV. CSt, R and CS are cumulative discounted coefficients for storage, EOR revenue and social costs across L periods from startt.

- B_t is commissioning-period allocation of whole-life commitments. It does not by itself reconstruct future annual cash expenditure; Eq8/14/26 enforce each scenario, not only expected storage costs.

- Omega scenarios, p_omega probabilities, Omega_N sampled set. iid scenarios are joint draws; independence between different sinks is not documented. D_k,omega and St_k,omega both appear as storage-realization notation.
