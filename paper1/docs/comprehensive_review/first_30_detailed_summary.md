## Findings relevant to Paper 1

- Cumulative geological resources, prescribed injection schedules and history-matched examples do not by themselves supply pressure-constrained annual injection limits. None of these 30 closes that dataset requirement.
- No reviewed item supplies a verified complete, matched-case capture-to-ship balance covering heat, electricity, compression, liquefaction, cooling and utility suppliers. Partial process duties and equipment ratings retain their original boundaries.
- Captured, injected, stored, avoided and permanently retained CO₂ are distinct quantities. Reported process improvements and economic savings cannot become whole-chain climate credits without the missing inventory and counterfactual.
- Dynamic network planning, uncertain-capacity design, multimodal transitions and reservoir/infrastructure coupling already exist in the literature. Paper 1 must acknowledge that prior work and state its contribution precisely.
- The strongest independent reconstructions here are the aggregate pinch-analysis cascade, regional aquifer resource accounting, published decision-tree inference and three hypothetical single-phase well columns. They verify those components under disclosed conditions, rather than full field forecasts or author optimizers.

**Study and reported results.** A 36 MW LNG cruise-ship study compares amine scrubbing, cryogenic capture, membranes and adsorption. Amine and cryogenic designs pass the stated integration screen. Both target 95% capture, but reported avoidance is 85.7% and 77.5%, respectively.

**Audit findings.** Table 7 duties are per kilogram avoided, which cannot be substituted for a captured-mass denominator. LNG cold and waste heat are essential case assumptions. Operational GWI omits a complete upstream, shipping, injection and permanence inventory. Noncrossing marginal CDFs do not establish that paired technology rankings never reverse.

**Independent checks and reproduction limits.** Printed cost identities, unit conversions, necessary cold-duty conditions and a probability counterexample were checked. Aspen/APEA, detailed heat integration, hazard calculations, weighting and the authors' Monte Carlo simulations were not reproduced.

**Use in Paper 1.** Supports discussion of capture versus avoidance and case-dependent integration. Hold its duties, sustainability ranking and lifecycle interpretation outside accepted Paper 1 inputs.

**Open evidence.** The explicitly referenced publisher supplement, process conditions, exhaust data, weights and native cases remain unavailable locally.
