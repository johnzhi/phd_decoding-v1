## Variables, units and assumptions

- AS amine scrubbing; A3C advanced cryogenic capture; MS membrane separation; PSA pressure-swing adsorption. These are selected configurations, not every possible technology variant.

- Common95% exhaust capture differs from85.7/77.5% case avoidance and from total-system GWI reductions. No denominator substitution or permanence credit.

- VOO m3; tank filling≤90%; pumps/compressors neglected only for the volume KPI. Ten-day storage basis is not a validated port schedule or annual capacity.

- CAPEX/FCI/TDPC/TIPC/TEC/EC in euros; OPEX euro/year. i=.08, n=25years. Printed FOPEX is3% of annualised capital, not ordinary3%CAPEX/year.

- PIF_GW=1; e_EG/e_capt/e_dir labelled tCO2/year; EF=.413kg/kWh, electrical loads kW, annual operation6264h. GWI graphs kt_eq/year. Explicit hours and mass conversion required.

- Waste heat supplies the AS reboiler; LNG139–293K provides1.28MW cold. Reference22.6bar saturated-liquid storage and5K exchanger approach. Pressure basis and complete stream states missing.

- Table7 duties MJ/kgCO2 AVOIDED, not per captured tonne. MS/PSA n.a. duty entries remain null, not numeric zero. Their avoidance is reported0%, without raw negative/unclipped results.

- MS0.95bar denotes transmembrane difference, not absolute pressure. Different separation/regeneration/storage pressures cannot be merged.

- HI uses LOC credit factors1/year, squared damage radius and worst scenario per LOC. Distance prose saysm; Table4 labelsm2. HI m2/year is a hazard proxy, not social acceptance or individual risk.

- NI and OSI are relative scores; weights depend on IN/EG/HI/EW perspectives. SF is a radar-area score; it is not kgCO2e or a unique physical objective.

- Uniform±20%KPI ranges and one million draws are assumed uncertainty, not empirical confidence calibration. Weight sampling, dependence, normalisation, seeds and joint records unavailable.

- 36MW full-load design,6264h/year and10days/voyage. Lack of secondary-load/time/layout/tonnage detail acknowledged; full-load design does not independently validate every lower-load heat/cold balance.

- LH2 and NH3 green-fuel benchmarks have100% operational avoidance in Table10. Fuel production, electricity origin, ammonia N2O and hydrogen supply leakage are not established by the zero-carbon label.
