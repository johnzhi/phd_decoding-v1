## Variables, units and assumptions

- x has2Nb pressure/saturation states;u hasNu well controls. The native case uses one rate-controlled well and6877 inferred cells. Geometry, units and functions are not reconstructed.

- Sw+Sg=1 andpg=pw+pc(Sg). Primary pressure phase, capillary closure and datum must match native code. A distributed phase-source rate is not automatically the same quantity as a volumetric well control.

- b is reciprocal formation volume factor, with rho=b*rho_surface. Eq30 prose describes the inverse ratio. Surface/reservoir states must be defined before well-flow conversion.

- K is intrinsic permeability, kr relative permeability, mu viscosity andlambda_alpha=kr/mu phase mobility. Peaceman total mobility uses a training average, not every individual phase mobility.

- Scalar g is gravity and bold g the residual;phi is porosity andPhi the POD basis;nu is specific volume and bold v the MILP variables;bold w contains binaries. Similar glyphs remain distinct.

- J/A/B differentiate the residual with respect to current/previous states and controls. MRST rate control requires a well-equation conversion delegated to SI.

- Phi is the pressure/saturation block basis;xi contains signed reduced states. Native lp=71, lsat=1, l=72. Basis signs are arbitrary;free reduced coordinates need explicit MILP-domain mapping.

- Psi=J*Phi is the chosen Petrov-Galerkin basis;Jr=(JPhi)^T(JPhi), Ar=Psi^TAPhi andBr=Psi^TB. Rank/conditioning matters;zero projected residual does not imply zero full mass residual.

- Training indices i/j differ from new timestep n. Interior searchS_n={n−1, n, n+1};initial/final clipping andj+1 availability require native implementation.

- The original distance uses reduced saturation and current cumulative volume. The MILP uses training-normalized cumulative volume alone. Epsilon0.001 needs compatible units/scaling;it is not a physical error bound.

- a is the sign binary, dpos/dneg nonnegative components, q one-hot selection anddmin the minimum. Products and selected maps require finite native bounds and exact indicator/disjunctive encoding.
