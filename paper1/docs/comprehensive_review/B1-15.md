## Variables, units and assumptions

- Intrinsic permeability k [m2], porosity phi and relative permeability kr are correlated calibration quantities. MK/Mphi are multipliers of native fields, not absolute measurements or annual-rate factors.

- nw/nCO2 are dimensionless phase-relative-permeability exponents, distinct from B1-14’s electrical Archie exponent. Residual saturations and kr endpoints are dimensionless model inputs.

- The two Eq1 denominators differ. Clipping, negative-base handling, phase appearance, hysteresis and explicit pc(Sw) are missing; paired component evaluation stays on the shared real physical domain.

- Kozeny-Carman phi0=.2 is a reference basis. Case3 Mphi=.5555 gives .1111 only at that basis, not a homogeneous field porosity. MK is dependent, not a fourth independent fitted parameter.

- Literal pressure gradient1.14 Pa/m stays on HOLD without a replacement. Initial62 bar at639.5 m, observations620 m and sensor550 m are distinct references; gauge/absolute basis is not stated.

- Table1 temperature34°C at639.5 m, .03°C/m gradient and the5 K/week cold-injection experiment have different scopes. Isothermal pressure fitting does not reproduce thermal feedback.

- SCAL, geological variograms and poro-perm mapping are case-specific. Generic DuMux GPL and ECLIPSE2009.2 metadata do not provide native files or commercial software rights.

- Directional near/far modifiers and the DuMux Kv/Kh1/3 parameter use different property contexts. Unknown baseline tensors prevent direct physical-anisotropy comparison.

- Nearfield1000×900 m, its enlargement and barrier150×20×5/6 m are chosen model structures. Native permeability, masks and case aliases are missing. Better arrival fitting is not geological identification.
