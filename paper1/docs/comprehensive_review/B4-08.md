## Variables, units and assumptions

- CO2 density: kg/m3, taken from the reported calculator at reservoir P/T. Calculator version/EOS, pressure basis and fluid composition remain unreproduced.

- Mass: kg in equations, Mt/Gt in results; 1 Mt=1e9 kg, 1 Gt=1e12 kg. Mt/year is a flow, distinct from stock Mt.

- Vg: initial gas at standard conditions, Sm3; tables use Tcf. Bg: m3/Sm3, rounded to three decimals. Standard T/P and unrounded factors missing.

- Vo: OOIP at standard conditions, Sm3; tables use MMbbl. Bo: reservoir m3/standard m3. Conversion and formation factor apply once.

- R and R_CO2: percentages. Table 3 generic primary/secondary and enhanced recovery factors coexist with Appendix current recovery; exact field selection unresolved.

- MMP: empirical pressure output unit unresolved; T in Eq4 must be Fahrenheit. API gravity threshold ≥27 for EOR; storage alone does not establish EOR eligibility.

- E: oil/gas storage coefficient assumed one because of limited data. It assumes recovered hydrocarbon voidage is available, without a field-specific pressure/sweep simulation.

- A: basin area; h: NET sand thickness; phi: 15% or 20%. Assumed depth ≥1,000 m, seal integrity and net-to-gross 0.3 are screening assumptions. h is already net.

- E_sa: 0.51/2/5.5 percent, not fractions. Generic ranges are not calibrated probabilities for these eight basins.

- Power emissions use factors × capacity ×5,000 h/year; refinery estimates use 330 days/year, 95 lb/bbl→0.043 t/bbl and stated specific gravity 0.86. No complete capture heat/electricity inventory.

- Authors call oil/gas contingent and aquifers prospective, citing SPE2017. Neither label independently establishes allocated commercial capacity or Japanese access.

- P10/P50/P90 labels accompany low/mid/high assumptions; statistical sampling, correlations and field-specific uncertainty calibration are not established.

- Distances are approximate basin/field values with different referents. Pipeline presence and contract expiry do not establish engineering reuse or rights.

- N=128 plants, 57 oil/gas fields and eight basin-aquifers; these are not measured injection or captured-flow time series.
