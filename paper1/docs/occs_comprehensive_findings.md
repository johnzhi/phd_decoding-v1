## Utility and environmental boundaries

The case is a 36 MW LNG cruise ship, with 6,264 operating hours/year and ten-day voyages. The AS design assumes recovered exhaust heat fully supplies its reboiler. CO2 liquefaction uses approximately 1.28 MW of LNG cold energy, with a 5 K approach and a reference saturated-liquid storage pressure of 22.6 bar. This integration does not establish available heat/cold on an ordinary bulk CO2 carrier.

Table 7 reports **MJ per kg CO2 avoided**, not per kg captured:

| Concept | Capture | Avoidance | Thermal MJ/kg avoided | Electricity MJ/kg avoided |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| AS | 95% | 85.7% | 3.55 | 0.95 |
| A3C | 95% | 77.5% | 12.14 | 1.98 |

The electricity unit conversion gives 263.8889 and 550 kWh/t **avoided**. These values do not become per-captured inventory inputs. Equation 14's exhaust-emission notation also needs reconciliation with the explicit captured-mass term in Equation 10; complete baseline/capture/auxiliary flows are unavailable. The reported operational GWI is not a complete lifecycle inventory including upstream/non-CO2 emissions, ports, transport, geological injection and retention.

Table 8's MS/PSA cold duties are 9.296875 and 6.421875 times the available LNG duty, supporting necessary rejection conditions for those selected designs. AS/A3C passing a total-duty comparison does not independently reproduce a detailed heat-exchanger/pinch design or operation across every engine load.

## Printed cost convention and units

The [independent diagnostics](../outputs/comprehensive_review/B1-01_diagnostics.csv), generated by [audit_occ_sustainability.py](../scripts/audit_occ_sustainability.py), preserve the printed equations:

- CAPEX is 2.86425 times total equipment cost.
- Equation 8 defines annual fixed OPEX as 0.03 times **annualised** CAPEX. With Equation 2's matching annuity factor, its lifetime present value is exactly **0.03 CAPEX**. NPC excluding variable OPEX is therefore 1.03 CAPEX. This is not replaced by the different convention of annual OPEX equal to 3% of CAPEX. The cited Feenstra original and author implementation remain unchecked.
- Equation 11's raw `EF × kW` has kg/hour units. At the printed 0.413 kg/kWh and 6,264 h/year, one kW corresponds to 2.587032 t/year. Explicit time and mass conversions are required before comparing annual GWI quantities.
- Equation 12 defines damage distance in metres, then squares it; Table 4 instead labels the distance m². Both are preserved pending clarification. The hazard-area index is a safety proxy, not measured public acceptance or a probability of injury.

## Ranking and robustness

Table 9 supports the reported individualist OSI ratio of 0.95/0.38 = 2.5. It does not provide the raw KPI inputs, weighting vectors or all-five-system OSIs needed for the complete rankings. Uniform ±20% KPI ranges and sampled weights are modelling assumptions, not empirically calibrated probabilities.

Page 12 argues that noncrossing Figure 9 cumulative-distribution curves imply no AS/A3C ranking inversions. Marginal CDF ordering alone is insufficient. As an analytical counterexample, independent **A ~ Uniform(0, 0.8)** and **B ~ Uniform(0.2, 1)** have noncrossing CDFs but **P(A > B) = 0.28125**. This probability belongs solely to the analyst example; it is not an estimate for OCCS or an error count in the authors' simulations. Paired SF differences or joint simulation traces are needed to verify their claim. Figure 6 directly plots paired ΔOSI, which is a different evidential basis.

Keep the cruise-ship case and its environmental/utility basis explicit.
