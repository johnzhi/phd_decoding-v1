## Questions preventing full reproduction

**Annual injection limits are missing.** The article describes independent annual upper bounds `kr,t` derived from well simulations. The supplement's pressure/efficiency/rate curves do not supply the complete 8-reservoir × 30-year bound array, pressure/time paths, fluid/well cases or scenario-specific interference treatment. The heatmaps contain chosen allocations `mr,t`. Using them as `kr,t` would turn outputs into constraints and predetermine part of the result.

**Early SW-A conservation needs clarification.** If all 5.327 Mt/year of Table S1 source mass is fixed, and only the reported 2.05 Mt/year can be injected initially, a network with same-year conservation and no escape or inventory cannot be feasible. The shortfall is 3.277 Mt/year under those conditions. The printed formulation does not specify the source-selection, curtailed-capture, venting or backlog stock treatment needed to reconcile this with SW-A. This is a question about the available specification, not proof of a defect in unpublished code. No carbon fate is assumed for the shortfall.

**The printed network formulation is incomplete.** The five Table 1 rows show source, hub and junction balances, annual reservoir bounds and readiness. Reservoir inflow equality is stated in prose. Explicit binary domains, arc/capacity/activation-cost links, a cumulative resource constraint and complete segment cost/design inputs are not provided. The wide objective lists route costs without the selection/flow links needed to implement them. Exact units and annual versus project-cost treatment also need clarification. Supplement Table S3 contains hub distances, not every required node-pair distance.

**Cost bases do not yet reconcile.** Figure 11's full-chain average exceeds the sum of its three displayed component averages by 1.92/3.54/3.90 USD/t. Weighting and denominators may differ, so these differences are not silently assigned to an invented cost component. The source-rate-weighted Table S1 capture cost is 94.44293 USD/t, compared with Figure 11's 93.85/93.76 averages. The undiscounted SW-A total-cost/stored-mass ratio is 114.4181 USD/t, compared with its displayed levelized full-chain average of 116.37 USD/t. Timing, discounting, included costs and version consistency must be established before comparing these as one inventory.

**Some discussion claims need a narrower interpretation.** Page 10 calls r3 quickly fully utilised, while the final SW-A map shows r2 at 100% and r3 at 44%; both are preserved pending clarification. Both hub-share charts show pronounced transitions, and no quantitative stability metric or transient hydraulic model is defined. Under fixed source volumes, increasing capture cost adds a constant to the routing objective, so an unchanged routing optimum in that sensitivity test is mathematically expected; it does not establish robustness to flexible capture or every uncertainty.
